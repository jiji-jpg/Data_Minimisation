Data Minimisation for Healthcare providers

Medicare and Government Data Asset
Medical and Government data assets include any health-related information your organisation collects, stores, or processes. This may include:
  • Residential and aged care records (e.g., care plans, incident reports, medication charts)
  • Medicare and insurance records (e.g. medicare benefits schedule claims, pharmaceutical benefits scheme claims, proof of vaccination documents.)
  • National Health registers (e.g. australian immunisation register, australian organ donor register, proof of vaccination documents)
