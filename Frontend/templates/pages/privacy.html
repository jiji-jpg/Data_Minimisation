Data Minimisation for Healthcare providers

Please read the following Privacy Notice:
Your Privacy Matters
We are committed to protecting your privacy and complying with applicable data protection laws. This notice explains how we handle your information during this questionnaire.

What information we collect
To calculate your data minimisation score accurately, we temporarily process your questionnaire responses during your active session only. We do not permanently store or collect any personally identifiable information.

Our Data Practice
All questionnaire data is held temporarily in your browser session memory only. Your data is automatically and permanently erased when you:
  • Close the questionnaire window or tab
  • Complete and exit the questionnaire
  • Experience a session timeout

Important Notes
  • Please complete the questionnaire in one session, as your progress cannot be saved or retrieved
  • All questions must be answered unless marked as optional to ensure accurate scoring
  • No data is transmitted to or stored on external servers
