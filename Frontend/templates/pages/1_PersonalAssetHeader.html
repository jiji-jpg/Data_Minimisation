Personal Data Asset
Personal data refers to any information that can identify an individual, either directly or indirectly. It includes details that relate to a person's identity, characteristics, or personal life.

In simple terms, if a piece of information can be used — alone or combined with other data — to recognize or contact someone, it is considered personal data.

Examples of Personal Data:
  • Basic identifiers: Name, address, phone number, email address
  • Government-issued identifiers: Passport number, driver's license number, tax file number
  • Online identifiers: IP address, usernames, social media handles, cookies
  • Demographic details: Age, gender, date of birth, nationality
  • Financial information: Bank account details, credit card numbers
  • Health or biometric data: Medical records, fingerprints, facial recognition data
  • Employment information: Job title, salary, employment history
