Data Minimisation Assessment Report

Healthcare Provider Data Privacy & Compliance Analysis

Executive Summary

This assessment evaluates the organisations data collection, retention, and management practices against data minimisation principles and relevant privacy obligations, including the Australian Privacy Principles (APPs) and My Health Act.

Data Minimisation Score is a metric that measures how well an organisation adheres to the principle of collecting and processing only the minimum amount of personal data necessary to achieve a specific purpose. Specific details will be mentioned below.

  • Low Score: Excessive data minimisation; immediate action required.
  • Medium Score: Adequate data minimisation, however, requires improvements.
  • High Score: Excellent data minimisation.

Data Retention Score is a metric that evaluates how effectively an organization implements appropriate time limits for storing personal data, ensuring information is not kept longer than necessary.

  • Low Score: Poor data retention; immediate action required.
  • Medium Score: Adequate data retention, however, requires improvements.
  • High Score: Excellent data retention.
