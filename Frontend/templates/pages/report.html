Data Minimisation Assessment Report

Healthcare Provider Data Privacy & Compliance Analysis

Executive Summary

This assessment evaluates the organisations data collection, retention, and management practices against data minimisation principles and relevant privacy obligations, including the Australian Privacy Principles (APPs) and GDPR. The organisation achieved a Data Minimisation Score of 6.8 and a Data Retention Score of 7.2, indicating a generally sound approach with identifiable opportunities to further reduce risk. Eight data asset categories were reviewed, with three priority areas identified where data is collected or retained beyond necessity, increasing regulatory and cybersecurity exposure. Four targeted recommendations are provided to support lawful, proportionate data use, strengthen retention controls, and improve accountability, while maintaining operational effectiveness.

Key Findings

The assessment identified generally sound data handling practices within clinical systems. However, gaps were observed in the collection, retention, and disposal of non-essential data, increasing privacy and regulatory risk. These findings highlight opportunities to strengthen compliance, reduce unnecessary data exposure, and improve operational consistency.

Personal Data Collection

Review all personal data fields collected across systems to confirm necessity for healthcare delivery or legal compliance. Remove or restrict the collection of non-essential personal data unless a documented purpose and explicit consent are in place.

Health Data Retention

Align health data retention periods with My Health Record guidance and Australian Privacy Principles by formally defining retention schedules for each data category and enforcing them consistently across systems.

Automated Deletion Controls

Implement automated data deletion or archiving controls based on approved retention schedules to reduce reliance on manual processes and ensure consistent enforcement of retention rules.

Purpose Limitation & Data Justification

Introduce mandatory documentation of data purpose and justification at the point of collection, supported by periodic reviews to confirm ongoing necessity and relevance.

Data Retention & Deletion

Action Required

This section assesses whether data retention periods are defined, justified, and consistently enforced, and whether deletion controls effectively reduce unnecessary data exposure.

Extended Retention Periods

Certain data categories are retained beyond recommended timeframes outlined in My Health Record guidance and relevant privacy principles. Extended retention increases exposure in the event of a data breach and may limit the organisations ability to demonstrate compliance with minimum necessary retention standards.

Recommended Actions

The following actions are prioritised to reduce regulatory and cybersecurity risk while maintaining operational continuity. Timeframes reflect practical implementation effort and risk severity.

Review Personal Data Collection Form

Conduct a structured review of all patient intake and administrative forms to confirm the necessity of each data field. Remove or make optional any non-essential fields and document the purpose for all retained personal data.

Complete within 2 weeks

Implement Consent Management

Introduce consistent consent capture mechanisms for sensitive and non-essential personal data. Ensure consent evidence is securely stored and auditable.

Complete within 1 month

Review and Formalise Retention Schedules

Define and document retention periods for each data category in alignment with applicable obligations and clinical requirements. Apply schedules consistently across all systems.

Implement within 6 weeks

Review Data Retention Schedule

Introduce automated deletion or archiving processes linked to approved retention schedules to reduce reliance on manual intervention and improve enforcement consistency.

Complete within 3 months
